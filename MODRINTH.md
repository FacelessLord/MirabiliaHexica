A fan-made addon for [Hex Casting](https://modrinth.com/mod/hex-casting). It started as a quality-of-life mod, but leaned more and more into new content, heavily inspired by *Ra* by qntm.

Made with heavy help from an AI coding assistant. A fan project, not affiliated with qntm.

## Features

- **Tanako's World**: a glass world reached in sleep. Cold, silent, with Lost Dreamers, ruined castles, listening posts, spells you have to find, and the Glass Man.

<!-- screenshot: dream-landscape -->

- **Ambient media**: spent media lingers. Too much makes hexes misfire and lets the dream pull you in. Machines discharge it, collect it or crystallize it.

<!-- screenshot: machines -->

- **Imbued items**: lenses that write a hex's results over your view, armor that runs a hex while worn, tools that cast one when used.

<!-- screenshot: lens-labels -->

- **Also**: wellsprings and tethers, macros, strings with chat reading and a mind palace, the Greater Lens, phials, wards, frost, and a sign on the sun.

Everything can be switched off or retuned in `config/mirabilia.json`, and the guidebook quotes your numbers.

English and Russian. This is a beta: expect rough edges, and please report them.

## Dependencies

On Fabric:

- Hex Casting 0.11.4
- Architectury API
- Fabric Language Kotlin

On Forge:

- Hex Casting 0.11.4
- Architectury API
- Kotlin for Forge

Trinkets or Curios are optional, for wearing lenses in a face slot.
